Method

A wall, in cross-section

i.

Survey

We visit, measure every room and photograph every flaw, then send you an itemised estimate: area, coats, gallons and labour, line by line.

ii.

Prepare

Protection first. Then filling, sanding, caulking and spot-priming. This is most of the job, and it is why the finish lasts.

iii.

Paint

Brush-cut edges and rolled fields, two full coats as standard. The same crew from first day to last.

iv.

Reveal

Tape off, furniture back, a walkthrough with you in daylight. Touch-ups the same day. Leftover paint labelled for you to keep.

Questions

Before you ask

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We visit, measure and send a written, itemised quote as soon as possible. There is no obligation and no pressure.

How long will my job take?

One or two rooms usually take a day or two; a whole-house interior three to six days; exteriors three to seven, depending on weather. Your quote gives exact dates.

Do I need to move furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture and put it back. Just put away small valuables and anything fragile.

What paint do you use?

Professional-grade paint from major brands, chosen for the surface. If you have a brand or an exact colour in mind, we'll use it.

How does payment work?

We take Zelle, card, check or cash. The payment details for your job are agreed in your written estimate, before any work starts.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Interior work is covered for 3 to 6 months, depending on the job: if paint peels, blisters or flakes because of how we applied it, we come back and fix it at no charge.

Can you work while we live or trade there?

Yes. We work room by room, keep a path clear and clean up every evening. Commercial jobs can be done overnight or at weekends.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance, so your home or business is covered while we work.

How do you leave the place clean?

We cover floors and furniture before we start, sweep and tidy every day, and walk through the finished job with you so everything is as we found it, only freshly painted.

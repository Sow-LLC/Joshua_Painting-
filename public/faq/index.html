Questions

Before you ask

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We visit, measure and send a written, itemised quote as soon as possible. There is no obligation and no pressure.

How long will my job take?

One or two rooms usually take a day or two; a whole-house interior three to six days; exteriors three to seven, depending on weather. Your quote gives exact dates.

Do I need to move furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture and put it back. Just put away small valuables and anything fragile.

What paint do you use?

Professional-grade paint from major brands, chosen for the surface. If you have a brand or an exact colour in mind, we'll use it.

How does payment work?

We take Zelle, card, check or cash. The payment details for your job are agreed in your written estimate, before any work starts.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Interior work is covered for 3 to 6 months, depending on the job: if paint peels, blisters or flakes because of how we applied it, we come back and fix it at no charge.

Can you work while we live or trade there?

Yes. We work room by room, keep a path clear and clean up every evening. Commercial jobs can be done overnight or at weekends.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance, so your home or business is covered while we work.

By service

Interior painting

How long does an interior job take?

One or two rooms usually take one to two days. A whole-house interior takes three to six days, depending on ceilings, trim and repairs.

Do you paint ceilings and trim too?

Yes. Walls, ceilings, baseboards, door and window casings and doors can all go in the same quote, or be priced separately.

Can we stay in the house while you paint?

Yes. We work room by room, keep a path clear, use low-odour paint and clean up every evening.

Exterior painting

When is the best time of year to paint outside?

Late spring to early fall is ideal, but we paint year-round whenever the surface is dry and the forecast is clear. Rain days are rescheduled at no cost.

Do you power-wash first?

Always. Washing removes dirt, chalk and mildew so the new paint bonds. Loose paint is scraped and sanded, and bare wood is primed.

Can you paint just the trim or one side of the house?

Yes. We do full exteriors and smaller jobs like trim, doors, a fence or the side that gets the most sun.

Cabinet painting

How long does a cabinet job take?

Most kitchens take four to six days, including drying time between coats. You can still use the kitchen in the evenings.

Will the paint hold up?

We degrease, sand, use a bonding primer and finish with a hard-wearing cabinet enamel, so it resists chips and daily wiping.

Can you change the hardware?

Yes. We fill the old holes and drill for new handles if you choose them before we start.

Wall repair & texture

Can you match my wall texture?

Yes. We match orange peel, knockdown and smooth finishes so the repair disappears once it's painted.

Do you remove wallpaper?

Yes. We strip it, wash off the glue, repair the wall underneath and prime it for paint.

Is repair work charged separately?

Small repairs are included in a painting quote. Bigger repairs are priced by the hour or as a flat price, agreed before we start.

Waterproofing

How often should a deck be sealed?

Most decks need a fresh coat of sealer or stain every two to three years, sooner if they sit in full sun.

Do you fix leaks?

We seal and coat surfaces to keep water out. If we find rot or a structural leak, we'll show you and tell you who to call.

Can waterproofing be done with an exterior paint job?

Yes, and it usually saves time and money because the washing and prep are shared.

Commercial painting

Can you work after hours?

Yes. Evening, overnight and weekend work can be scheduled so your business doesn't close.

Do you paint between tenants?

Yes. We turn rentals and multi-unit buildings around quickly so units are ready to show.

Can you quote several locations at once?

Yes. Send the addresses and we'll visit and price each site in one written quote.

Ready for a free estimate?

Tell us about the job and we'll come out, measure and send you a written price. No cost and no pressure.
